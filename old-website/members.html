THE NUCLEAR MATERIALS GROUP MEMBERS

PhD Candidate Researchers

Chase Gesteland

Chase is a 3rd year PhD student researching additive manufacturing of materials for extreme environments, including steels, titanium alloys, and tungsten.

Angela Luanne Thevenin

My research with Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory (LLNL) and UC Berkeley focuses on developing advanced high-entropy alloys (HEAs) for plasma-facing components in next-generation fusion reactors.

Yuxin Hu

Yuxin Hu is a PhD student in Mechanical Engineering at UC Berkeley studying fracture mechanics and microstructure–property relationships in metallic structural materials. His research combines electrodeposition of nickel-based systems and additive manufacturing with fracture toughness testing, advanced microscopy, and finite-element modeling to understand crack behavior across length scales.

Daphne Michelle Lucana

Daphne is a second-year PhD student in the Materials Science and Engineering (MSE) department at UC Berkeley, with a B.S. in MSE from Cornell University (2023). Her research focuses on micromechanical properties of nuclear materials. Outside the lab, she enjoys concerts, running and exploring matcha cafes.

Sarah Ellis

Sarah is a first year PhD student studying micromechanical properties of nuclear reactor materials, especially silicon carbide. She is experienced with the femtosecond laser, microtensile testing, nanoindentation, and microscopy. Before coming to Berkeley, Sarah graduated from Vanderbilt University with a B.E. in Chemical Engineering and Climate Studies in 2025. Outside of the group, Sarah likes to do CrossFit workouts, play board games, and spend time outdoors.

Myeongjun Lee  ​

Myeongjun Lee is a Materials Science and Engineering PhD student from SNU, Korea. His research is mainly focused on metallic glasses, nanomechanical properties, and Helium ion irradiation response.

Lucy Strazis

​Lucy is a first year PhD student and Rickover Fellow working on mechanical testing of oxidized grain boundaries of structural materials in PWR environments. Her research interests include fundamental corrosion mechanisms and the relationship between crystal structure and material properties. Before UCB, she received a BSE from the University of Pittsburgh in Chemical Engineering with minors in French and Computer Science. Outside of the lab, Lucy likes live music, hiking, and reading.

Kevin Lam

​My research is in the development and characterization of additively manufactured stainless steel lattices for shock absorption applications. 

Undergraduate Researchers 

Samik Krishnan

Samik is a Chemical Engineering and Nuclear Engineering student at UC Berkeley (Class of 2028) with strong interests in reactor hydraulics, nuclear materials, nuclear physics, and fission systems. He works on both the mechanical and software aspects of engineering systems related to nuclear and energy technologies.

Shahin Alaei

Shahin Alaei is a senior studying Nuclear Engineering and Materials Science. His work focuses on nuclear fuel and structural materials, extending structural material lifetimes through laser annealing, and investigating the interplay between irradiation and corrosion. Through summer internships, he has also gained experience in inertial fusion energy target fabrication, including work with foam aerogels. Outside the lab, he enjoys skiing, playing soccer, and cooking.

​

Aidan Elkins

Aidan Elkins is a 3rd-year Nuclear Engineering undergraduate interested in materials for fusion energy. He primarily conducts micromechanical testing and materials characterization on tungsten alloys using nanoidentation and SEM imaging. In his free time, he plays IM basketball and is on a dance team.

Kavin Ram

Mechanical Engineering Major. Research Interests: materials for extreme aerospace/nuclear environments, refractory high entropy alloys.
